Six ways to train smarter

Which training platform fits the way you ride?

Athlima, TrainerRoad, HumanGO, JOIN, RestOrTrain and ChatGPT can all support better training, but they make very different promises. Some take over the planning. Some turn your devices into a connected coaching ecosystem. Athlima puts workout execution, local data and practical coaching directly in your browser.

The market at a glance

LOCAL, OPEN & HANDS-ON

Athlima

A browser-based training cockpit for riders who want to plan, ride and review without handing their full training life to another cloud account.

  • Runs structured cycling workouts and controls compatible smart trainers
  • Local rule-based coaching for cycling and running
  • Workout library, calendar, FIT recording and Intervals.icu connection
  • No Athlima login, no subscription and self-hosting is possible

Best fit: privacy-conscious riders, tinkerers and anyone who wants maximum control with minimum subscription overhead.

CYCLING PERFORMANCE ENGINE

TrainerRoad

A polished, performance-first system built around one central promise: make cyclists faster with adaptive, power-based training.

  • AI selects and adapts workouts from training history, performance and recovery
  • AI FTP Detection, fatigue prevention and daily recommendations
  • Indoor and outdoor delivery through Zwift, Garmin, Wahoo and more
  • Interactive calendar and personalized performance insights

Best fit: committed cyclists who want a mature, highly automated path to stronger race performance.

MULTISPORT AI COACH

HumanGO

A conversational, AI-led endurance coach designed around busy schedules, changing availability and ambitious single- or multisport goals.

  • Hugo adapts plans using training, availability, feedback and health metrics
  • Running, cycling, triathlon and duathlon support
  • Wearable connections, guided workouts and workout delivery to supported watches
  • Premium adds multiple goals, recalibration tests and race pacing tools

Best fit: runners and multisport athletes who want a personable AI coach that reshapes the plan around real life.

SIMPLE, FLEXIBLE CYCLING

JOIN

A cyclist-friendly coaching app that hides the complexity and gives you a clear next step based on your goal, level and available time.

  • Adaptive plans react to availability, completed rides and missed sessions
  • More than 400 workouts and plans for building, improving and peaking
  • Workout player, workout score and easy-to-understand JOIN level
  • Connections with services and devices including Strava, Zwift, Garmin and Wahoo

Best fit: cyclists with busy calendars who want professional structure without feeling like they are operating a sports-science dashboard.

CONNECTED ENDURANCE COACH

RestOrTrain

A managed coaching experience that combines computed training metrics, AI guidance, season planning and workout delivery.

  • Calculates training values before AI analysis and adds endurance-specific guardrails
  • Builds seasons, phases and individual workouts
  • Automatic activity and recovery sync from supported services
  • Workout delivery plus an indoor FTMS player with ERG

Best fit: endurance athletes who want coaching decisions and workout delivery in one connected service.

FLEXIBLE GENERAL ASSISTANT

ChatGPT

A broad AI assistant rather than a dedicated training platform. Its strength is flexible thinking, explanation and analysis across almost any topic.

  • Can discuss plans, interpret uploaded files and explain training concepts
  • Useful for brainstorming, reflection and turning context into readable advice
  • Not a dedicated workout calendar, sensor platform or smart-trainer controller
  • Quality depends heavily on the prompt, supplied data and available tools

Best fit: athletes who want a versatile thinking partner and are happy to bring their own data and workflow.

Choose by the experience you want

“I want to ride, not manage another subscription.”

Athlima is the clearest fit. Your core training data stays in your browser, trainer control is built in and you can self-host it.

Start Athlima

“My main goal is becoming a faster cyclist.”

TrainerRoad offers the deepest performance-focused automation. JOIN is the more approachable alternative when simplicity and schedule flexibility matter more than a heavy performance system.

“I train for running or triathlon too.”

HumanGO stands out for multisport goals and an AI-coach relationship. RestOrTrain is compelling when connected metrics and workout delivery are the bigger priority.

“I want advice beyond the training app.”

ChatGPT is the broadest thinking partner. Use it alongside a dedicated platform when you want explanations, reflection or help connecting training with travel, nutrition planning or everyday life.
