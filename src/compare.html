  • Outdoor delivery and connected workflows including Zwift, Garmin and Wahoo

MULTISPORT AI COACHING

HumanGO

Build endurance training around your real schedule with Hugo, an AI coach that adapts as your availability, fitness and feedback change.

  • Cloud-based web and mobile coaching for running, cycling and multisport goals
  • Plans adapt using availability, physiology, feedback and health metrics
  • Connections include Garmin, Strava, Zwift, Wahoo, Peloton and Apple Health
  • Premium adds multiple goals, recalibration tests and a race-planning tool

Essential: $16.99/month or $155.99/year. Premium: $28.99/month or $264.99/year. Free trial offered.

SIMPLE ADAPTIVE CYCLING

JOIN

Know what to do next without getting lost in sports-science complexity. JOIN reshapes your cycling plan around your goal and available time.

  • Adaptive plans respond to availability, completed rides and missed sessions
  • More than 400 workouts, a workout player, workout score and JOIN level
  • Plans for building fitness, improving specific abilities and peaking for events
  • Sync with Garmin, Wahoo, Zwift, Strava, TrainingPeaks and Hammerhead

€16.99/month or €119.99/year; 7-day free trial for new users

Choose JOIN for professional cycling structure in a straightforward, friendly package.

CONNECTED ENDURANCE COACH

RestOrTrain

Move from training data to a season plan, daily recommendation and delivered workout inside one connected endurance service.

  • Calculates training metrics before AI analysis and applies endurance-specific guardrails
  • Plans cycling, running, swimming and triathlon seasons and workouts
  • Syncs supported activity and recovery data automatically
  • Delivers workouts to supported devices and includes an FTMS indoor player with ERG

A few questions each month are free. US App Store Pro: $14.99/month or $134.99/year; regional prices vary.

Choose RestOrTrain for end-to-end endurance coaching, planning and delivery.

GENERAL AI ASSISTANT

ChatGPT

Bring your own questions, files and context to a broad AI assistant that can explain, analyze and brainstorm far beyond sport.

  • Can discuss training ideas and analyze uploaded files

  • Not a dedicated training calendar, workout player or smart-trainer controller
  • Results depend on the prompt, supplied context and enabled features

Free plan available. ChatGPT Plus: $20/month; API usage is separate.
