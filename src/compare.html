Training tools compared

Athlima vs RestOrTrain vs ChatGPT

These three tools solve different parts of training. Athlima is a free, open-source browser app for planning and executing workouts locally. RestOrTrain is a subscription endurance-coaching service with automatic sync and delivery. ChatGPT is a general-purpose assistant that can reason about training context you provide.

Local & open source

Athlima

Best for riders who want trainer control, workout execution and deterministic local coaching without an account or cloud backend.

Integrated coaching service

RestOrTrain

Best for athletes who want synchronized history, adaptive planning and workout delivery handled as a managed service.

General-purpose AI

ChatGPT

Best for broad questions, explanations and flexible analysis when you supply or connect the relevant training context.

Which one should you choose?

Choose Athlima if…

You value local data, self-hosting, open source, direct trainer control and a cycling-first workout screen. It is also the only option here that combines a browser workout player with a fully local rule-based coach.

Start Athlima

Choose RestOrTrain if…

You want a managed coaching experience that automatically syncs training and recovery data, adapts plans and sends workouts to supported devices.

Choose ChatGPT if…

You want a flexible general assistant for explanations, brainstorming and analysis across training and unrelated topics, and you are comfortable supplying the necessary context.
